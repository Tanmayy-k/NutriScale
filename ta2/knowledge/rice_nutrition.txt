TITLE: White Rice — Nutrition Reference

White rice (cooked) is a staple carbohydrate source widely used in recipes worldwide.

Calorie density: approximately 1.3 kcal per gram (cooked weight).
Macronutrients per 100 g cooked:
  - Calories: 130 kcal
  - Carbohydrates: 28.2 g
  - Protein: 2.7 g
  - Fat: 0.3 g
  - Fibre: 0.4 g

Brown rice (cooked) has slightly more fibre and a similar calorie density (~1.1-1.4 kcal/g).
Basmati rice is similar in calories to white rice (~1.3 kcal/g cooked).

Common serving sizes:
  - 1 cup cooked rice ≈ 186 g → approximately 242 kcal
  - A typical restaurant portion ≈ 150-200 g → 195-260 kcal

Storage: Cooked rice should be consumed within 1-2 days if refrigerated.

DISCLAIMER: These are reference estimates sourced from general nutrition databases.
They are not medically precise values and should not be used for clinical dietary planning.
