TITLE: Pasta — Nutrition Reference

Pasta (dry, wheat-based) is a versatile carbohydrate-rich ingredient.

Calorie density:
  - Dry pasta: approximately 3.5 kcal per gram (before cooking)
  - Cooked pasta (boiled): approximately 1.3-1.6 kcal per gram (absorbs water)

Macronutrients per 100 g dry pasta:
  - Calories: 350 kcal
  - Carbohydrates: 70 g
  - Protein: 13 g
  - Fat: 1.5 g
  - Fibre: 2.5 g

Macronutrients per 100 g cooked pasta (plain, no sauce):
  - Calories: 131-158 kcal
  - Carbohydrates: 25-31 g
  - Protein: 5 g
  - Fat: 0.5-1.0 g

Whole-wheat pasta (cooked): ~1.4 kcal/g (slightly higher fibre content).
Egg noodles (cooked): ~1.38 kcal/g.

Common serving sizes:
  - 1 cup cooked pasta ≈ 140 g → approximately 196-221 kcal
  - A typical restaurant portion ≈ 200-250 g → 262-395 kcal

Note: Pasta sauce adds significant additional calories.
Plain tomato sauce adds roughly 30-50 kcal per 100 ml.
Cream-based sauces can add 150-250 kcal per 100 ml.

DISCLAIMER: These are reference estimates sourced from general nutrition databases.
They are not medically precise values and should not be used for clinical dietary planning.
