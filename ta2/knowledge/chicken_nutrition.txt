TITLE: Chicken Breast — Nutrition Reference

Chicken breast (boneless, skinless) is a lean protein source commonly used in recipes.

Calorie density: approximately 1.65 kcal per gram (cooked, grilled or baked).
Macronutrients per 100 g cooked:
  - Calories: 165 kcal
  - Carbohydrates: 0 g
  - Protein: 31 g
  - Fat: 3.6 g
  - Sodium: 74 mg

Raw chicken breast has slightly fewer calories (~1.1 kcal/g raw weight) because it loses
water during cooking and concentrates nutrients.

Common serving sizes:
  - 1 medium chicken breast ≈ 120 g cooked → approximately 198 kcal
  - A restaurant chicken portion ≈ 150-180 g → 248-297 kcal

Chicken thigh (skinless, boneless, cooked): ~2.1 kcal/g (higher fat content).
Chicken with skin adds roughly 50-80 kcal per 100 g serving.

Safe cooking: Chicken must be cooked to an internal temperature of 74°C (165°F) to be safe.

DISCLAIMER: These are reference estimates sourced from general nutrition databases.
They are not medically precise values and should not be used for clinical dietary planning.
